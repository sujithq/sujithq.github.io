Microsoft FORGE Lab researchers used codenamed MDASH to study Linux kernel vulnerabilities and reported 59 upstream bugs, with 33 CVEs assigned and 38 fixes merged. The post also notes eight local root privilege-escalation exploits and discusses the difficulty of validating AI-generated findings and getting fixes upstream.
