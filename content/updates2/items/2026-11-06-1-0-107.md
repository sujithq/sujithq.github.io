Release v1.0.107 adds many CLI, terminal, sandbox, MCP, model-picker, and session-management fixes. Notable changes include inline image support in Rex terminals, a new /code command, a unified /settings dashboard with global search, and numerous corrections for Windows, Unicode, control-character handling, resumable sessions, and authentication flows.
