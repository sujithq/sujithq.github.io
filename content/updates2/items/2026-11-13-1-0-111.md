Release v1.0.111 adds new permission-rule regex support, a one-time remote host session creation flag, and several MCP/plugin/workflow/session fixes. It also includes many terminal UI, rendering, and reliability fixes across /usage, /diff, /resume, search, paste handling, and startup/sign-in flows.
