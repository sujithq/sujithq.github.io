Azure Static Web Apps will retire support for .NET 8 and .NET 9 on November 10, 2026. Managed Functions APIs should be moved to .NET 10 before then; existing apps will keep running, but security updates and support will stop.

- **Source:** [Azure Updates](https://azure.microsoft.com/updates?id=575106)
