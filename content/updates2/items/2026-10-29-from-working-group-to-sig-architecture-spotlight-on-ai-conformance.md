Kubernetes moved AI Conformance from a temporary Working Group into a permanent SIG Architecture subproject after establishing the initial requirements and certification process. The change is meant to keep AI infrastructure requirements vendor-neutral and aligned with Kubernetes architecture as AI workloads become more common.

- **Source:** [Kubernetes Official Blog](https://kubernetes.io/blog/2026/10/29/sig-arch-ai-conformance-2026/)
