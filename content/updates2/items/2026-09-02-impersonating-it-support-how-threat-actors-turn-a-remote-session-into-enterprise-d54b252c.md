Microsoft Threat Intelligence reported a human-operated intrusion campaign that abuses Microsoft Teams external collaboration to impersonate IT support, gain remote access, and deploy a Node.js-based implant. The campaign uses social engineering and legitimate tools to move from initial access to lateral movement, with Microsoft Defender used for detection and disruption.
