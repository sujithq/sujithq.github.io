Microsoft Threat Intelligence reported a sharp rise in email-based phishing activity in Q3 2026, while QR code phishing declined. The post also noted a rebound in CAPTCHA-gated phishing and highlighted risks from malicious messages spoofing familiar business-service sender domains and security override behavior.
