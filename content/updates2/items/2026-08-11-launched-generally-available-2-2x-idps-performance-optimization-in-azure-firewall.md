Azure Firewall Premium now supports up to 22 Gbps throughput with TLS inspection and IDPS in Deny mode, up from 10 Gbps. It also increases per-TCP-connection throughput with IDPS enabled in Alert or Deny mode to 600 Mbps, from 300 Mbps.
