Release v1.0.106 adds inline image support in Rex terminals, a new /code command, unified /settings/dashboard navigation, and several session/agent management improvements. It also includes many fixes for Windows, MCP/LSP/sandbox behavior, prompt editing, terminal rendering, and help/docs accuracy.
