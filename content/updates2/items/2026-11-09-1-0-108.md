Release v1.0.108 adds numerous CLI behavior fixes, help-text updates, and MCP/autopilot improvements. Notable changes include better session/resume handling, more consistent permission and tool-call behavior, improved search and transcript rendering, and default installation/activation for managed plugins marked `defaultEnabled`.
