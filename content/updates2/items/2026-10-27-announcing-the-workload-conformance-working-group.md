Kubernetes announced a new Workload Conformance Working Group to define a shared, testable standard for workload behavior at runtime. The group will produce a per-minor-version specification, objective tests, and upstream conformance tooling, with initial coverage focused on runtime behavior rather than application logic.

- **Source:** [Kubernetes Official Blog](https://kubernetes.io/blog/2026/10/27/announcing-workload-conformance-wg/)
